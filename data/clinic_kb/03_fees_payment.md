# Fees and payment

## Consultation fees
Dr. Ayesha Malik: Rs. 2000. Dr. Usman Tariq: Rs. 1500. Dr. Sana Iqbal (child specialist, Saturday): Rs. 2500.

## Follow-up fee
A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 7 days of the first visit costs Rs. 500. After 7 days the full consultation fee applies.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer. Card machines are not available yet.

## Senior citizen discount
Patients aged 65 and above get 20% off the consultation fee. Please bring your CNIC.

## Procedure charges
BP and blood sugar check: Rs. 200 (free with consultation). ECG: Rs. 1000. Nebulization: Rs. 500. Wound dressing: Rs. 400 to Rs. 800 depending on size. Injection administration: Rs. 200.
