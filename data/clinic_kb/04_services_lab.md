# Services and lab

## Services offered
General checkup, fever and flu treatment, BP and diabetes monitoring, ECG, nebulization for asthma and breathing trouble, wound dressing, stitches removal, injections and drips, vaccination for children and adults, medical certificates for school, university and jobs.

## Services not offered
We do not offer X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan, MRI, surgery, or overnight admission. For these, the doctor will refer you to a hospital or imaging centre.

## Vaccinations
We give routine EPI childhood vaccines, flu vaccine (seasonal), typhoid vaccine, hepatitis B vaccine and tetanus. Please bring the child's vaccination card. Vaccination is by appointment, preferably in the morning shift.

## Lab tests
Blood sample collection for lab tests (CBC, sugar, HbA1c, lipid profile, LFT, RFT, thyroid, urine test, dengue and typhoid tests) is done at the clinic from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, through our partner lab Al-Noor Diagnostics. The lab counter stays open during the 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM doctor break. Most reports come the same day by evening; thyroid and HbA1c take 24 hours. Fasting tests need 10 to 12 hours of fasting.

## Home sample collection
Home sample collection is available in Johar Town, Wapda Town and Model Town for Rs. 300 extra. Book it by phone at least 3 hours in advance.

## Medical certificates
Fitness and sick-leave certificates are issued only after the doctor examines the patient in person. Fee is the normal consultation fee.
