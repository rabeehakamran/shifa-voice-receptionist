# Shifa Family Clinic — About

## Clinic overview
Shifa Family Clinic is a general physician (family medicine) clinic in Johar Town, Lahore. We treat adults and children for common illnesses such as fever, flu, cough, stomach problems, blood pressure, diabetes follow-ups, allergies and minor injuries. For specialist problems our doctors refer patients to the right specialist or hospital.

## Address and location
Address: 14-B, Main Boulevard, Block G, Johar Town, Lahore (near Akbar Chowk, opposite the Shell petrol pump). The clinic is on the ground floor. Landmark in Urdu: "Akbar Chowk ke qareeb, Shell petrol pump ke saamne".

## Parking and accessibility
Free car and bike parking is available in front of the clinic. The clinic is on the ground floor with a ramp, so it is wheelchair accessible.
