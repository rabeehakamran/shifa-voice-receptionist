# Policies

## Appointments and walk-ins
Appointments are recommended. Walk-in patients are also seen, but patients with appointments get priority. Each appointment slot is 20 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes early.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
You can cancel or reschedule an appointment any time by calling. There is no cancellation fee. If you are more than 15 minutes late your slot may be given to a walk-in patient.

## Medical advice over the phone
The receptionist cannot give medical advice, diagnosis, or medicine doses over the phone. Please book an appointment so a doctor can examine you. In an emergency call Rescue 1122 or go to the nearest hospital emergency.

## Reports and privacy
Patient information, reports and appointment details are private. They are only shared with the patient in person, never over the phone with another person.

## What to bring
Please bring your CNIC (or B-form for children), previous prescriptions, old reports and a list of current medicines.

## Emergencies
Shifa Family Clinic is not an emergency centre. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, unconsciousness, severe bleeding, seizures, stroke symptoms or poisoning, call Rescue 1122 immediately or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
